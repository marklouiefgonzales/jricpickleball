About Johneric

Johneric picked up his first paddle on a borrowed court and never put it down. He built this place so anyone in the community can play without needing to know the rules, own the gear or bring a full group.

Our approach is simple: keep games friendly, keep courts well kept, and help every player leave a little better than they arrived.

  • Certified coaching for beginners to advanced
  • Well-kept courts with proper lighting
  • Friendly, welcoming community

Questions players ask

Do I need to bring my own paddle?

No. We have paddles and balls to borrow for open play and lessons.

I've never played. Can I still join?

Yes. Beginner sessions are the best place to start, and Johneric will walk you through the rules.

How do I book a court?

Send us a message below or call. We'll confirm your slot within the day.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and non-marking court or indoor shoes.
